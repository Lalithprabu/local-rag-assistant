Sourdough bread rises using a natural culture of wild yeast and bacteria instead of commercial yeast.
A sourdough starter needs regular feeding with flour and water to stay active, usually once a day at room temperature.
The long fermentation time gives sourdough its characteristic tangy flavor and chewy texture.
Baking sourdough at high heat in a covered pot, known as a Dutch oven, helps create a crisp, well-risen crust.
